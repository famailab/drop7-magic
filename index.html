      HOW TO PLAY
      • Drop discs into the 7 columns — tap, drag, or use ← → + Space.
      • A disc pops when its number matches the number of discs in its row or column.
      • Chains multiply points: 7 pts × chain per disc.
      • Gray discs are blanks. When a disc pops next to one, it cracks — a second neighboring pop reveals a hidden number, turning it into a normal disc!
      • You get 5 discs per level. When they run out, the board rises and a cracked row appears at the bottom.
      • Combos earn bonus discs — the first pop of a turn earns nothing, but every chain wave from ×2 on grants +1 disc! Magic drops end the level, but a big combo can earn it back.
      • Clear the whole board for a fireworks celebration!
      • Game over when a disc is pushed above the top — or when the board is completely full and the next disc has nowhere to drop.
      • SETTINGS (title screen): color themes, LCD wallpaper, and your own brand name!
      MAGIC BALLS
      • Glowing magic balls sometimes appear as your next ball. Each is consumed when dropped — and dropping one ends the level immediately!
      • Magic is smart: it shows up more often when the board is crowded (a lifeline!), rarely when the board is empty — and never on an empty board. No blanks around? You won't get PEEK.
      • ± SHIFT — adds −3…+3 to every numbered ball (set by column, left to right). Numbers stay within 1–7.
      • 👀 PEEK — reveals the hidden numbers of every blank in the chosen column. Slide to peek first!
      • ⟳ CYCLE — sliding ←/→ cycles every ball: blank → cracked → numbered → blank. Drop to lock it in.
      • ◉ UNIFY — turns every numbered ball into one number — pick 1–7 with the highlight column.
